# Missing Features Analysis (Hackathon Market)

Based on a thorough analysis of the current CodeVerse/Hackathon Market application structure and research on leading real-world platforms like Devpost and HackerEarth, here is a practical evaluation of the platform's current state and a list of crucial missing features needed to compete in the real world.

### 1. Current State Analysis
Your application already has a solid foundational architecture with entities handling:
* Role Management: (Users, Types, Organizers, Admin Dashboard)
* Hackathon Lifecycle: (Hackathon creation, Categories, Applications, Submissions)
* Team Dynamics: (Teams, Team Members)
* Judging Workflow: (Judge Assignments, Judge Scores)
* Monetization & Tracking: (Payment Transactions, Notifications, Audit Logs)

This covers the essential administrative and logistical backbone of running a hackathon. 

### 2. Important Missing Features (Real-World Practicality)

To make this platform highly competitive for developers and organizers, consider implementing these missing features:

#### A. Engagement & Community Features
* Public Project Gallery & Portfolios: Allow participants to showcase their past projects globally. Once a hackathon ends, projects should be searchable, filterable by tech stack, and commentable to foster post-event recruitment.
* Smart Team Formation / Matchmaking: An automated matching system where lone participants can find teams based on missing skills (e.g., a "Frontend Developer" looking for a "Backend Developer" or "UI/UX Designer").
* In-app Chat & Discussion Forums: Real-time chat channels built into the platform (or seamless Discord/Slack integration APIs) for participant coordination and organizer announcements.

#### B. Advanced Submission & Evaluation Validation
* Built-in Code/Submission Validation: Integration with GitHub/GitLab APIs to authenticate repositories. The platform should be able to verify if a repository was created *during* the hackathon timeline to prevent cheating.
* Live Leaderboards: A real-time updating dashboard during the judging phase so participants can see standings or popular votes (Community Choice Award).

#### C. Event Logistics & Mentorship
* Mentor Booking System: Participants often need help during hackathons. A feature where teams can "request a mentor" by raising a ticket, categorized by tech-stack, and active mentors can join their virtual room or claim the ticket.
* Schedule & Integration: An interactive timeline view for the event, integrating calendar invites for workshops, keynote speeches, and submission deadlines.

#### D. Analytics & Exporting (For Organizers/Sponsors)
* Sponsor Portal & Resume Drop: Companies sponsor hackathons to hire talent. A portal where sponsors can easily filter, download resumes, and see the tech stacks of opted-in participants.
* Post-Event Analytics: Dashboards showing organizers participant drop-off rates, most used technologies, and engagement metrics (valuable for proving ROI to sponsors).

### Recommendations for Immediate Roadmap
If you are planning to add features soon, prioritize the Smart Team Formation and the Public Project Gallery, as these directly impact the user experience of participants and provide long-lasting SEO/Growth benefits for the platform.
